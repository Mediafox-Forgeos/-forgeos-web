| 4   | **StopTransaction race** — a `StopTransaction` lands between a recovery attempt's conflict check and its resume write                                                                                | Low                                                                                                                                  | Pre-CAP-006A: the stale `resumeSession` call would throw on a fresh read of `COMPLETED`, silently dropping the `SESSION_RECOVERED` audit event (PR27 audit §3, R-06)                                                                                                         | The entire conflict-check-through-resume sequence is now one atomic transaction under the connector lock; a `StopTransaction` racing it either completes fully before or fully after the recovery transaction (never interleaved), and the recovery call's own fresh re-read after the lock correctly reports `already-resolved` if the session finished first  | The audit-event gap (R-06) is now structurally impossible for this specific interleaving — the two operations can no longer partially interleave, only fully serialize one way or the other                                                                                                                        |
| 5   | **Server restart** — `movos-api` process restarts while sessions are `ACTIVE`/`OFFLINE` and connections are live                                                                                     | High over any long enough operational timeframe (deploys happen)                                                                     | Pre-CAP-005: no reconciliation at all. | 9   | **Mass disconnect** — thousands of stations disconnect together (a regional outage, a shared upstream router failing)                                                                                | Medium — the scenario Objective 3 exists specifically to answer                                                                      | Pre-CAP-006A: `sweepStale()` fires one unawaited `handleConnectionClosed` call per stale connection, all at once, in a single synchronous loop tick — an unbounded burst against the database precisely when the system may already be under stress from the incident itself | The same `ConcurrencyLimiter` from scenario 2, applied uniformly: at most 25 concurrent notifications in flight, the rest queued. ## Reading this matrix

Rows 1, 3, and 4 are now closed to a materially higher standard than before (proven via the connector advisory lock, not merely argued). Rows 2 and 9 are bounded, not eliminated — the queue absorbs bursts up to whatever total volume the deployment actually needs to handle; this document does not claim a specific throughput ceiling was load-tested beyond what CAP-006A_VALIDATION.md's runtime evidence covers. Rows 5–8 and 10–11 are either unchanged from CAP-005 (correctly, since they weren't this work order's target) or inherit their residual risk directly from a still-open, explicitly-named limitation (the fixed recovery window, the billing-completeness question) rather than from anything CAP-006A left unexamined.
